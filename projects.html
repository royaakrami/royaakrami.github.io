NASA Goddard Space Flight Center · Mar 2025 — Feb 2026

Exospheric H-Lyman-alpha Mapping

Processed H-Lyman-alpha intensity data in JupyterHub and MATLAB through a 28-layer residual neural network to model and predict future emission patterns. The work, using data from the Nancy Grace Roman Telescope to advance exospheric mapping methods for the Carruthers Geocorona Observatory, is the basis of a paper in development and was presented at the American Geophysical Union 2025 conference.

Python · MATLAB · JupyterHub · ResNet · NASA datasets
